The Logistics, Planning, and Organizing (LPO) Committee is responsible for logistics, planning, and organizing chapter events and meetings including the quarterly chapter meetings and the annual chapter convention; events and activities in coordination with other committees, the branches, and working groups. The LPO Committee also creates and implements best practices for managing office supplies, materials, and other resources. All committees, working groups, and branches report and coordinate through the LPO Committee.

The LPO generally meets every two weeks on Monday at 7:30PM, usually on Zoom.
