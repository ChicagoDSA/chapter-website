Most of our organizing happens through our committees. These include permanent standing committees, project committees (i.e. priority campaigns), and working groups (i.e. looser committees organized around common interests, institutions, or substantive areas of work).

All Chicago DSA members are encouraged to join a committee. The work of these committees promotes strategies and advances tactics that bring forth a working-class-centered politics of humanity and solidarity, affect positive change in Chicago’s neighborhoods, and build relationships with other organizations with similar goals.

Join a committee today, and check out our events page for upcoming meetings.
